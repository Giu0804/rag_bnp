Let $x_i$ be the factual segment studied. Let $\mathcal{C}_i$ be the set of chunks initially cited by the LLM for this segment $x_i$. Let $f(x_i, \hat{x}_j)$ be the overall hybrid match score calculated for the segment $x_i$ and a candidate chunk $\hat{x}_j$.

We define an adjustment factor $\gamma > 0$: $\gamma < 1$ is a tolerance factor and $\gamma > 1$ is a strictness factor.



\paragraph{Mean validation}
We calculate the mean of the scores of the chunks $\mathcal{C}_i$. The acceptance threshold is defined by:
$\tau_{\text{mean}} = \gamma \cdot \frac{1}{\vert \mathcal{C}_i \vert} \sum_{\hat{x}_k \in \mathcal{C}_i} f(x_i, \hat{x}_k)$. We accept citations whose score is above this threshold.

\paragraph{Max validation}
We determine the maximum score among the chunks $\mathcal{C}_i$. The acceptance threshold is defined by
$\tau_{\text{max}} = \gamma \cdot \max_{\hat{x}_k \in \mathcal{C}_i} f(x_i, \hat{x}_k)$. This is the strictest configuration

\paragraph{Min validation}
We determine the minimum score among the chunks $\mathcal{C}_i$. The acceptance threshold is defined by:
$\tau_{\text{min}} = \gamma \cdot \min_{\hat{x}_k \in \mathcal{C}_i} f(x_i, \hat{x}_k)$. This is the most flexible configuration.

Finally, for all three configuration, we have: 

$$\mathcal{C}^*_i = \mathcal{C}_i \cup \left\{ \hat{x}_j \notin \mathcal{C}_i \mid f(x_i, \hat{x}_j) \geq \tau_i \right\}$$

From an experimental standpoint, we performed a parameter exploration on $\gamma$ and $\lambda$ for the three scenarios above.
In particular, we tested:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item $\gamma \in [0.4, 1.7]$ with a step of 0.05
  \item $\lambda \in [0.1, 1]$ with a step of 0.05
\end{itemize}
